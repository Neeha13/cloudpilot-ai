# User Research Plan

## Research objective
Understand how small engineering teams investigate cloud cost increases and decide which optimization work to prioritize.

## Recruiting
Aim for 5–8 participants:
- 2–3 cloud/DevOps engineers
- 1–2 startup CTOs or engineering managers
- 1–2 FinOps practitioners if available

## Interview questions
1. Tell me about the last time you investigated a cloud bill that was higher than expected.
2. What triggered the investigation?
3. Walk me through what you did, step by step.
4. Which tools, reports or people did you rely on?
5. What was the hardest or slowest part?
6. How did you decide whether a resource was safe to optimize?
7. What happened after you found an opportunity?
8. How did you estimate or verify savings?
9. What information was missing when you had to make a decision?
10. What would make you distrust an optimization recommendation?

## Avoid
- "Would you use an AI tool?"
- "Would this dashboard save you time?"
- Pitching the solution before understanding the current workflow.

## Synthesis template
For each interview, record:
- Participant role and company size band (no confidential company details)
- Recent incident/story
- Current workflow
- Pain point and frequency
- Impact (time, money, risk)
- Workaround
- Evidence / exact quote (with permission)
- Confidence level

## Evidence standard
Do not state that a problem is validated until multiple independent participants describe a similar recent behavior or pain point. Separate observed evidence from interpretation.
